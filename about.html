ABOUT US

We believe payments
should just work.

No matter where you are. No matter how weak your signal is. OneBar is building the backup layer India's payment system needs.

OUR MISSION

Making digital payments
as reliable as cash.

India processes 20 billion UPI transactions every month. But 18% of them fail — mostly because of bad networks. That's millions of people pushed back to cash every single day. We're building the technology to fix that.
