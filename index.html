HOW IT WORKS

Works like any UPI app.
Just never fails.

Same flow as PhonePe or GPay. The difference?
OneBar.in has a backup that kicks in the moment signal drops.

01

Open OneBar.in

Enter amount, scan QR or type phone number. Identical to any UPI app you already use.

02

Signal drops? App switches.

OneBar.in detects loss of internet and automatically switches to offline mode. You don't do anything.

03

Payment goes through.

Sent via Bluetooth or sound wave. Both phones get confirmation. Settles to bank when back online.

WHY IT'S SAFE

Your money never
leaves your bank.

We know what you're thinking. Here's why offline payments with OneBar.in are completely secure.

Bank-linked, always

Zero wallet. Zero pre-loading. Your money stays in your actual bank account — we just move it.

One-time encrypted token

Every offline payment generates a unique cryptographic code that expires in 60 seconds.

RBI compliant limits

₹500 max per offline transaction. ₹2,000 per day. Exactly as per RBI's offline payment guidelines.

PIN every time

Your UPI PIN or biometric is required for every single transaction — online or offline. No exceptions.
